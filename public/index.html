A coordinated team of specialized AI agents that works every submission alongside the underwriter: scoring risk, stress-testing the portfolio and fingerprinting each submission, then handing over evidence-backed findings. Faster, sharper decisions, with a human in the loop on every call.

Multi-agentTool useEmbeddingsHuman-in-the-loopUnderwritingEnterprise
example agents

  • Portfolio concentration agent: stress-tests how each new risk shifts exposure across the book, surfacing accumulation before it's bound
  • Submission DNA agent: fingerprints every submission as a vector embedding and matches it against its nearest-N neighbors by cosine similarity, instantly surfacing identical submissions
